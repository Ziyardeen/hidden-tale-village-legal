Privacy Policy — Forge Posting

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Forge Posting is a private tool run by the owner of the Hidden Tale Village channels. It exists for one purpose: to upload the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token limited to the permission video.upload. It uses that token only to send the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, and to ask TikTok whether an upload succeeded.

What it stores, and where

The token is stored on the owner's own computer, in a private file. It is never sent to any other service and never shared with anyone.

What it does not do

It does not collect data about TikTok users, followers, viewers or visitors. It does not read anyone's videos, messages or profiles. It does not sell, share or analyse personal data. It uses no advertising or tracking.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and websites), and by deleting the token file.
